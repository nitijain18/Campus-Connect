Our Mission

About CampusConnect

CampusConnect is a smart event and vendor management platform built to connect students, event organizers, and colleges on one single hub.

🎓

For Students

Discover fests, workshops, and meetups across colleges near you. Book your seat, get a QR ticket instantly, and never miss out on campus life.

🎤

For Vendors

Apply as a vendor, get approved, and start hosting your own paid or free events. Track bookings, manage earnings, and reach students directly.

🏫

For Colleges

Give your campus community a single place to discover everything happening — technical fests, cultural nights, workshops, and more.
